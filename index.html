Places of Interest

Red Fort

The Red Fort, located in Old Delhi, is a historic fort built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in 1648. Made from striking red sandstone, it served as the main residence of the Mughal emperors for nearly 200 years. The fort is famous for its impressive walls, beautiful palaces, and Mughal-style architecture. It is an iconic symbol of India's rich history and hosts the Prime Minister’s Independence Day speech every year.

India Gate

India Gate is a war memorial dedicated to the soldiers of the British Indian Army who died during World War I. It is one of the most visited landmarks in New Delhi. It is a famous war memorial built in 1931 to honor the 70,000 Indian soldiers of the British Indian Army who lost their lives during World War I. Designed by architect Sir Edwin Lutyens, it resembles the Arc de Triomphe in Paris. The 42‑meter‑high monument is made of sandstone and has the names of thousands of soldiers inscribed on it. Beneath the arch once stood the Amar Jawan Jyoti, a flame lit in memory of unknown soldiers.

Lotus Temple

The Lotus Temple is a Bahaʼi House of Worship notable for its flowerlike shape. It is open to people of all religions and is a symbol of peace and unity. The temple has no idols or rituals; visitors simply sit in silence for meditation and reflection. Surrounded by gardens and pools, the Lotus Temple is one of Delhi’s most visited and photographed landmarks.

Akshardham Temple

The famous Akshardham temple in Delhi symbolizes admiration for widely understood Indian spirituality with its magnificent craftsmanship. It is formally called Swaminarayan Akshardham. Since its inauguration in 2005, Akshardham has been regarded as a beacon of tranquility and devotion. Hindu traditions spanning centuries are reflected in the intricate carvings, grand structures, and serene gardens of the temple. Standing out as one of the largest Hindu temples worldwide, Akshardham is not only dedicated to Bhagwan Swaminarayan’s teachings but also acts as a reminder of faith, harmony and compassion..

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, Near to Delhi India, is one of the most famous monuments in the world. IT is a major tourist atraction and attracts large number of foreign visitors. It was built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in the 17th century (completed in 1653) in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. The monument is often regarded as the greatest symbol of eternal love.

Famous Restaurant: Indian Accent

Indian Accent is a highly acclaimed fine‑dining restaurant located at The Lodhi, Lodhi Road, New Delhi, known for its inventive modern Indian cuisine that blends traditional Indian flavors with global ingredients and techniques. The restaurant has earned numerous awards and global recognition, including being featured for several years on Asia’s 50 Best Restaurants list and praised internationally for its creative tasting menus.
